## Profile

I build enterprise AI agents and applied machine learning systems, with experience across generative AI, NLP, deep learning, and computer vision. At ServiceNow, my work focuses on agent planning, orchestration, ReAct-style task execution, permission-aware tool use, and evaluation of agent behavior across task success, robustness, latency, cost, and model compatibility.

Previously at KLA, I worked on machine learning and computer vision for semiconductor defect inspection. I hold a PhD in Computer Science from West Virginia University, where my research focused on activity recognition, multi-view sensor fusion, and image classification.
